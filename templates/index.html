One Club, Every Branch
Heritage

Kept the same way since 1921

Founded at a single ground and now standing across several branches, Country Soccer Club has never treated its records as paperwork. Every member's arrival, every family's registration, every match played — entered once, and never lost.

A member who joined as a child and returns as a parent finds the same ledger, unbroken. That continuity, more than any trophy, is what the mark stands for.

Membership

Three ways to belong

I — Minor

Junior Member

$100 annually · Ages 4–17
  • Registered under one family member
  • Full access to club hobbies programme
  • Eligible for team formation from age 4
  • Progress record kept for life
II — Major

Senior Member

$200 annually · Age 18+
  • Full voting standing at club level
  • Eligible for every FIFA fixture
  • Transferable between branches
  • Instalment payment, up to four terms
III — Family

Family Patron

By registration · No fee
  • Registers and accompanies minor members
  • Relationship recorded — parent to guardian
  • May hold standing at more than one branch
  • Notified of every fixture by post
